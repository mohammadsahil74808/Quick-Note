# DEV MAVERICKS — AI / ML & TECHNOLOGY COMMUNITY
## Lingaya's Vidyapeeth | CSE Block
### INNOVATE • BUILD • DISRUPT

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**DEV MAVERICKS** is a student-led AI and technology community at **Lingaya's Vidyapeeth** focused on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI and emerging technologies. The club creates a practical ecosystem where students can explore technology beyond the classroom, develop real skills, build projects, participate in competitions and showcase their work.

The club follows a practical progression: **Explore → Learn → Build → Compete → Showcase**, supported by regular activities and a rolling three-month planning cycle.

## 4. Mission & Objectives
- **Introduce** students to AI, ML, Generative AI and emerging technologies.
- **Provide beginner-friendly opportunities** alongside advanced technical activities.
- **Encourage hands-on project development** and real-world problem solving.
- **Conduct at least one meaningful activity every month.**
- **Organize** workshops, build challenges, competitions, hackathons and technical sessions.
- **Develop** leadership, teamwork, communication and technical skills.
- **Create a platform** for students to showcase projects, achievements and innovation.
- **Build a sustainable technical community** within the CSE Block.

## 8. Weekly Friday Planning System
The core team holds a regular Friday planning/review meeting and maintains a rolling three-month roadmap.
- Review completed activities and outcomes.
- Track upcoming event preparation.
- Plan and refine the next three months.
- Discuss hackathons, workshops, challenges and collaborations.
- Assign responsibilities and deadlines.
- Review venue, permissions, resources and operational requirements.
- Coordinate website, LinkedIn and documentation updates.

## 9. Initial Activity Roadmap

### Activity 01 — INTRODUCTION TO AI
- **Date**: 21 August 2026 | **Time**: 2:00 PM | **Venue**: Room 2105, CSE Block
- **Theme**: *Explore. Learn. Innovate.*
- **Scope**:
  - Understand AI and ML basics.
  - Explore real-world AI applications.
  - Discuss how AI is used in everyday life.
  - Introduce practical AI learning paths.
  - Conduct interactive discussion and doubt-clearing.
  - Introduce the upcoming DEV MAVERICKS roadmap.

### Activity 02 — BUILD WITH AI
- **Timeline**: August 2026 — *Tentative*
- **Scope**: A hands-on challenge held during the first month, moving students from learning about AI to creating an AI-powered idea or prototype.
  - **AI for Campus**: solve a student or college-related problem.
  - **AI for Impact**: address a meaningful real-world or social problem.
  - **Crazy AI**: create an unexpected or experimental AI solution.
  - **Open Innovation**: build any meaningful AI-powered idea.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Idea & Team Formation $\rightarrow$ 2. Build Phase $\rightarrow$ 3. Prototype/Demo $\rightarrow$ 4. Pitch $\rightarrow$ 5. Feedback & Recognition

### Activity 03 — AI/TECH HACKATHON #1
- **Timeline**: September 2026 — *Tentative*
- **Scope**: Practical solutions using AI and related technologies.
  - AI & Automation
  - Computer Vision
  - Machine Learning
  - Generative AI
  - AI for Social Impact
- **Workflow**:
  1. Problem Statement Release $\rightarrow$ 2. Team Formation & Development $\rightarrow$ 3. Mentoring / Checkpoints $\rightarrow$ 4. Final Submission $\rightarrow$ 5. Live Demo & Pitch $\rightarrow$ 6. Evaluation & Winners
